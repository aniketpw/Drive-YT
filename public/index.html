🔐 Google Drive Permissions & Channel Destination Guide

1. Google Drive Access & Sharing Requirement

The Google account connected to this portal must have access to the Drive folder or video files. If the Google Drive folder belongs to a different account, you must either share the folder with your connected email address, OR set folder access to "Anyone with the link can view". If a folder is restricted/private and not shared with your connected account, Google Drive API will block access.

2. Which YouTube Channel receives the uploaded videos?

All videos are uploaded directly to the YouTube Channel belonging to the connected Google Account. If your Drive videos are on Account A, but you want them uploaded to Account B's YouTube Channel: simply share the Drive folder with Account B (or enable link sharing), and log in to this app using Account B. The cloud pipeline will read from Account A's Drive and stream directly into Account B's YouTube channel!

🛡️ 100% Privacy & Ownership Guarantee

🔒 Strict Per-User Data Isolation

Videos, upload histories, and job states are rigidly siloed per Google account. A zero-failure SQLite backend ensures absolute isolation between different channels and users.

🔑 Private API Quota & Targeted SSE

Monitors your dedicated 10,000 units/day API quota per session. Live dashboard updates (SSE) are securely routed only to the authenticated session owning the upload.

☁️ Zero Third-Party Video Storage

Videos stream in high-speed chunks directly between Google Drive servers and your YouTube channel via a zero-RAM pipeline.

👁️ Unlisted Video Default

Every single upload defaults strictly to Unlisted. Only people with the exact unlisted embed or video URL can view your media.
